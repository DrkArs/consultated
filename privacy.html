Consultated

Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Consultated collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no network connection.

What stays on your device

Your career progress, the name you type for your character, and your settings (volume, haptics, text speed) are saved inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. They never leave the device. They're included in your iCloud or computer backups if you have backups turned on, and deleting the app deletes them. You can also delete your career at any time in the game under Settings → Reset career.

What we collect

Nothing. The game makes no network requests, so it can't send data to us or to anyone else. We don't sell or share data because we don't have any.

Third parties

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity's analytics, diagnostics and hardware-statistics reporting are turned off in this game, so it sends nothing to Unity.

If you've chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. That data is collected by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and you can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

We collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
