Consultated

Climb the consulting ladder. Try not to fall off it.

A satirical career game for iPhone. Each week one message lands on your work phone; every reply moves four fragile meters: Client, Partner, Sanity and Integrity. Keep them balanced through a review cycle to get promoted or poached. Let one hit empty or full and you're fired.

Support

Is my progress saved?
Yes, automatically, on your iPhone only. It survives app updates and is included in your device backups.
How do I start over?
Open the Settings app inside the game and tap Reset career (it asks twice).
Can I see the tutorial again?
Settings → Replay tutorial. It plays on your next message.
There's no sound.
Check the volume sliders in the in-game Settings and that your iPhone isn't in silent mode.
Something else, or found a bug?
Write to millerriver01@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version.
